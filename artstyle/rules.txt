# Extra rules added to every image prompt (one per line). Lines starting with # are ignored.
# The art style itself is in style.txt; things never to draw are in avoid.txt.
Each image illustrates the meaning and feeling of its own line in a short reflective reel. Each scene can have its own people and setting; continuity between scenes is optional. Choose a specific everyday detail or a gentle visual metaphor that fits the line.
Vary subjects, settings and camera framing across the reel: an adult in an ordinary moment, a pair of adults, a close-up of adult hands, a meaningful object, an empty room or a quiet outdoor view. People are optional. Give every image one clear focal point and calm surrounding space.
Match the colored-pencil examples consistently: warm paper, visible pencil strokes, softly irregular contours, layered hatching, muted colors and simple vintage anime character design. Apply the pencil medium to the whole scene, including people and backgrounds.
Whenever humans appear, every person, including background figures, must be clearly a young adult aged 20 to 30. Use youthful adult facial features and adult body proportions. Avoid elderly features, grey hair, children, teenagers, school-age characters and childlike faces with oversized eyes. If the line recalls childhood, suggest the memory through objects, a setting or an adult reflecting on it.
Make adults approachable through natural gestures, recognizable feelings, simple clothes and ordinary life. Keep expressions subtle and unposed, with enough face and hand clarity to read on a phone.
Richness comes from a few carefully observed textures and lived-in details. Keep detail selective and leave quiet areas for the viewer's eye to rest. Avoid filling every surface with decoration or dense pencil marks.
Keep the center area calm for captions, using a wall, sky, water, soft shadow or distant background. Place the focal subject slightly to one side at middle height or higher. Keep faces, hands and essential objects above the bottom third so they remain clear when the radio overlay is enabled.
